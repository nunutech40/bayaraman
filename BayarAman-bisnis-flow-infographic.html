BayarAman: Bisnis Flow Rekber Concierge

🎯 Fokus MVP: Midtrans Payment, Payout Manual
BayarAman MVP memakai Midtrans untuk invoice/payment. Setelah payment confirmed, operator koordinasi via WhatsApp group, buyer konfirmasi pakai OTP, lalu operator melakukan pencairan manual ke seller.
1 FLOW BISNIS UTAMA
1🧾

Buat Transaksi

Seller atau buyer bikin transaksi di BayarAman.

2🏪

Seller Valid

Buyer-created flow wajib seller accept + verify rekening.

3💠

Invoice Midtrans

Backend create Snap invoice/payment session.

token / redirect_url
4💳

Buyer Bayar

Buyer bayar di halaman Midtrans.

5🔔

Webhook Status

Midtrans confirm payment ke backend BayarAman.

6💬

Group WA

Operator buat group buyer + seller + BayarAman.

7📦

Seller Kirim

Seller kirim barang/jasa setelah admin announce payment masuk.

8💸

Payout

Buyer OTP confirm, operator transfer uang ke seller.

⭐ Nilai bisnis utama: buyer bayar via invoice Midtrans milik BayarAman, seller kirim setelah payment confirmed, BayarAman jadi pihak penengah yang operasionalnya transparan.
2 DUA ENTRY POINT TRANSAKSI
🏪

Seller Sebagai Peng-input

  • Seller buat transaksi
  • Masukkan detail deal dan rekening seller
  • Share link ke buyer
  • Buyer bayar via Midtrans
🛒

Buyer Sebagai Peng-input

  • Buyer buat transaksi
  • Masukkan detail deal dan rekening seller
  • Seller wajib accept dan verify rekening
  • Buyer baru boleh bayar
🛡️

BayarAman Operator

  • Monitor payment confirmed
  • Buat WhatsApp group
  • Kirim link konfirmasi
  • Transfer payout ke seller
3 STATUS OPERASIONAL MVP
🎟️

Payment Session

Snap token/redirect URL dibuat.

⏳

Payment Pending

Buyer belum selesai bayar.

✅

Payment Confirmed

Webhook/status Midtrans sukses.

💬

WA Group Created

Trust room dibuat manual.

🔐

Buyer Confirmed

Buyer klik link dan lolos OTP.

💸

Paid Out

Operator transfer uang ke seller.

4 KONFIRMASI SELESAI & KOMPLAIN
1📦

Barang/Jasa Selesai

Seller dan buyer info di WhatsApp group.

2🔗

Admin Kirim Link

Link konfirmasi dikirim oleh operator di group.

3📨

OTP Dikirim

OTP ke email atau WhatsApp buyer.

4🔐

Buyer Confirm

Buyer input OTP untuk menyetujui release dana.

5⚠️

Kalau Ada Komplain

Buyer-seller selesaikan di luar sistem, via group/operator.

6📝

Outcome Dicatat

Release, refund, split, atau cancelled.

7🏦

Payout/Refund

Operator jalankan transfer sesuai outcome.

8📚

Audit Trail

Status, note, dan referensi pembayaran tersimpan.

⚖️ MVP tidak membangun dispute center dulu. Sistem cukup mencatat issue dan final outcome supaya operasional tetap bisa diaudit.
5 RISIKO & KONTROL MVP
🏦

Fake Payment

Kontrol: validasi webhook/status Midtrans, order_id, dan amount.

🏪

Salah Rekening Seller

Kontrol: seller wajib accept dan verify rekening.

💬

Operasional WA

Kontrol: group link, note, dan timestamp disimpan.

🔐

Konfirmasi Palsu

Kontrol: confirmation link + OTP buyer.

🐢

Payout Lambat

Kontrol: payout queue/status dan referensi transfer.

📉

Komplain Tinggi

Kontrol: catat issue/outcome untuk bahan Phase 2.

🎯

Goal Validasi MVP

Buyer percaya bayar ke BayarAman
Seller mau kirim setelah admin confirm
WA group cukup jelas
OTP confirmation dipahami
Fee model diterima
Beban operator terukur

🚀 Phase 2 Setelah Terbukti

Admin dashboard penuh, WhatsApp API, in-app dispute workflow, refund automation, dan payout/disbursement automation.

⭐ Ringkasnya: BayarAman MVP = transaksi tercatat, buyer bayar via Midtrans, koordinasi via WA, buyer OTP confirm, seller dibayar manual.
