## 2. Problem And Outcome

```text
Problem:
Buyer, seller, and admin need one trusted transaction record even though payment,
cancellation reconciliation,
fulfillment, WhatsApp coordination, risk review, and money transfers include manual
or outside-system work. Provider events, requests, and external messages must not silently
become verified financial facts or completed financial outcomes.

Expected user outcome:
Buyer and seller can start or join in the correct role, understand the current
trusted status and next actor, complete their required actions, and see whether
the transaction paid the seller, cancelled without funds, refunded the buyer,
split the funds, expired, or remains held for complaint, risk, or manual review.

Expected business outcome:
BayarAman enforces role ownership, deadlines, payout holds, and action eligibility;
records cancellation eligibility, every trusted manual result, and every money
movement; and preserves traceability without pretending that bank, WhatsApp, or
operational/compliance work is automated.
```

### Confirmed UI/UX Design Constraint

- BayarAman is delivered as a web application.
- Buyer, seller, and admin experiences use a mobile-app layout as the canonical presentation on mobile and desktop browsers.
- On a mobile viewport, the product uses the available screen width.
- On a larger browser viewport, the product remains a constrained mobile-width app surface and does not expand into a desktop dashboard.
- Exact maximum width, breakpoints, surrounding browser canvas, and component behavior must be specified and validated during UI/UX Design.

## 3. Actors And Goals

| Actor | Goal | Entry point | Successful end state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Account acting as seller | Start or join a physical-goods deal, request/respond to eligible cancellation, fulfill only after payment confirmation, and receive the agreed payout | Create transaction as seller, open seller invitation, or open eligible cancellation action | `PAID_OUT`, `CANCELLED`, `REFUNDED`/`SPLIT_SETTLED`, or clearly held/manual-review state |
| Account acting as buyer | Start or join a deal, pay BayarAman, request/respond to eligible cancellation, and authenticate receipt confirmation | Create transaction as buyer, open buyer invitation, payment/cancellation action, or confirmation link | `PAID_OUT`, `CANCELLED`, `REFUNDED`, `SPLIT_SETTLED`, `PAYMENT_EXPIRED`, or clearly held/manual-review state |
| Initiator | Create the shared deal, invite the opposite role, and cancel before counterparty join when eligible | Authenticated create-transaction or invitation-waiting action | Distinct counterparty joins, transaction cancels, or both role datasets become complete |
| Admin | Reconcile Midtrans exceptions, turn WhatsApp evidence into audited checkpoints, and perform authorized financial operations | Admin operation queue or transaction record | Required trusted result is recorded and only the valid next action remains available |
| Admin with internal risk/compliance assignment | Review prohibited-item, suspected-fraud, or policy holds without inferring a default financial outcome | Authorized `RISK_HOLD` queue/handoff | Hold remains or only the recorded authorized manual outcome becomes available |
| BayarAman system | Enforce participant, time, hold, and state rules | User/admin action or approved timer | Valid next state, rejection, expiry, hold, or terminal result is produced |

BayarAman has three product roles: Buyer, Seller, and Admin. `Initiator` describes how a Buyer or Seller starts a transaction; it is not a fourth account role. Risk review is also an Admin responsibility. Any Ops, Finance, Supervisor, or reviewer labels below describe internal Admin task assignments, not separate user-facing roles.

## 7. Business Rules

| Rule ID | Rule | Applies to | Failure/alternate outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `UR-BR-001` | One account may act as buyer or seller in different transactions, but one transaction requires two distinct accounts with opposite roles | Account/transaction roles | Same-account join is rejected |
| `UR-BR-002` | A WhatsApp number is mandatory before account participation in a transaction | Account onboarding | Creation/join remains blocked |
| `UR-BR-003` | Buyer and seller WhatsApp numbers are snapshotted per transaction and used for manual group creation | Transaction contacts | Number mismatch requires correction/recovery; no silent replacement |
| `UR-BR-004` | Buyer OTP is WhatsApp-only to the buyer transaction snapshot used for the group | Buyer confirmation | Delivery/retry remains on same number; no email or new-number fallback |
| `UR-BR-005` | Each actor authors only their own role data; seller alone owns authoritative payout data | Transaction input | Unauthorized actor cannot save authoritative value |
| `UR-BR-006` | Both role datasets must be complete before payment instructions and original deadline exist | Payable readiness | Transaction remains waiting for role data |
| `UR-BR-007` | Completing seller-owned data replaces a separate seller-acceptance action | Seller join | No extra accept/reject state is introduced |
| `UR-BR-008` | Buyer pays through the hosted Midtrans page; status becomes authoritative only at `settlement + fraud_status=accept` | Payment | Transaction stays non-authoritative until the validated provider event or Get Status API result |
| `UR-BR-009` | Original payment deadline starts at payable time and is 1x24 hours | Payment expiry | No timely claim produces `PAYMENT_EXPIRED` |
| `UR-BR-010` | Midtrans invoice availability starts the absolute 1x24-hour deadline; invoice/status retry never resets it | Payment expiry | Provider due_date follows when supported; late events never revive the transaction |
| `UR-BR-011` | Only validated Midtrans `settlement + fraud_status=accept`, not Buyer action or provider `capture`, is authoritative payment confirmation | Payment authority | Other provider results remain non-paid, waiting, or reconciliation-required |
| `UR-BR-012` | Admin must create the WA group and record payment announcement before seller fulfillment | Fulfillment readiness | Seller remains unauthorized to fulfill in the approved flow |
| `UR-BR-013` | Seller-complete and buyer-complete are separate admin-recorded checkpoints based on WA evidence | Completion | Link remains unavailable until both exist |
| `UR-BR-014` | Confirmation link creation starts a 2x24-hour buyer-response window; reminder is due after 1x24 hour | Buyer confirmation | No OTP after 2x24 hours becomes overdue without auto-release |
| `UR-BR-015` | Buyer silence never makes payout eligible automatically | Confirmation recovery | Payout stays held for OTP or controlled review |
| `UR-BR-016` | Controlled exception requires buyer-complete checkpoint, WA evidence, audited reason/operator/time, and no known complaint/hold | Admin exception | Missing condition produces manual review or complaint hold |
| `UR-BR-017` | Complaint recorded before payout processing creates mandatory `PAYOUT_ON_HOLD` and disables payout plus confirmation exception | Complaint | Funds remain held until written agreement outcome |
| `UR-BR-018` | Complaint negotiation and agreement occur outside system; BayarAman records but does not adjudicate | Complaint | No agreement remains `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` |
| `UR-BR-019` | Allowed written-agreement outcomes are full seller release, full buyer refund, or split settlement | Settlement | Other outcome requires upstream product revision |
| `UR-BR-020` | Eligibility, processing, and terminal financial success are separate states | Payout/refund/split | No terminal status before required success reference(s) |
| `UR-BR-021` | Seller release uses seller-owned transaction payout snapshot | Seller payout | Different destination is not silently substituted |
| `UR-BR-022` | Full refund requires one successful buyer transfer; split requires successful buyer and seller transfer records | Refund/split | Failure remains non-terminal and requires recovery |
| `UR-BR-023` | WhatsApp activity is never parsed into trusted status automatically in MVP | Manual WA operations | Admin must record every trusted checkpoint/outcome |
| `UR-BR-024` | Complaint reported after payout processing starts does not automatically stop or reverse transfer | Post-processing complaint | Handling remains outside approved pre-payout hold flow |
| `UR-BR-025` | Trusted manual and financial actions require audit actor, time, result, and applicable evidence/reference | Admin operations | Action cannot be treated as complete without audit record |
| `UR-BR-026` | One verified WhatsApp number belongs to one account; transaction WhatsApp snapshots freeze at payable time | Account/contact security | Account changes affect future transactions only; lost active number enters manual hold |
| `UR-BR-027` | An invitation is role- and transaction-bound, single use, valid for 3x24 hours, and revoked when reissued | Counterparty join | Wrong-account viewing does not consume it; same-account use is rejected |
| `UR-BR-028` | Shared deal, role, contact, payout, and refund data lock when both role datasets complete and payment becomes available | Agreement integrity | A pre-funding correction requires a replacement transaction; a funded correction enters manual hold pending an approved upstream route |
| `UR-BR-029` | MVP accepts only legal, shippable physical goods and excludes prohibited/regulated goods, live or perishable goods, digital products, services, financial instruments, and complex title-transfer transactions | Product eligibility | Ineligible transaction cannot become payable and may require operational review |
| `UR-BR-030` | Item price must be Rp100,000 through Rp5,000,000; buyer service fee is 2% of item price with Rp10,000 minimum and Rp50,000 maximum | Pricing | Out-of-range or invalid calculations cannot become payable |

| `UR-BR-032` | Buyer and seller financial destinations are participant-authored and frozen at payable time; admin cannot replace them | Refund/payout ownership | Any requested change enters manual hold or requires a replacement transaction |
| `UR-BR-033` | Only a validated authoritative Midtrans settlement for the exact frozen amount follows the normal payment-confirmation path | Payment review | Partial, excess, duplicate, delayed, unknown, or late provider outcomes cannot trigger group creation or fulfillment |
| `UR-BR-034` | A partial/provider mismatch remains under Admin reconciliation and cannot be treated as paid or used to reset the unchanged invoice deadline | Payment exception | Any supported refund follows the approved Midtrans/manual fallback route |
| `UR-BR-035` | Excess, duplicate, and late provider outcomes enter payment exception/reconciliation and any refund uses Midtrans when supported or the frozen buyer destination fallback | Payment exception | No affected event revives an expired transaction or authorizes fulfillment |
| `UR-BR-036` | OTP is six digits, valid for five minutes, resendable after 60 seconds up to three sends per 30 minutes, with five invalid attempts followed by a 30-minute lock | Buyer confirmation security | Only the newest code works; repeated delivery failure enters manual review on the same number |
| `UR-BR-037` | Buyer refund bank is required as buyer-role data before payable time and is masked/frozen like seller payout data | Refund readiness | Missing buyer refund data keeps the transaction from becoming payable |
| `UR-BR-038` | In a split, buyer portion plus seller portion equals item price plus shipping; service fee is outside the split pool and remains earned after payment confirmation except when BayarAman causes the refund | Split settlement | Invalid totals cannot be approved; buyer leg is attempted before seller leg |
| `UR-BR-039` | Admin task assignments may own WA checkpoints, Midtrans reconciliation, transfers, or approvals | Admin authorization | Assignment labels are not product roles; an Admin outside the assigned permission cannot perform the action |
| `UR-BR-040` | Controlled exceptions, refunds, splits, and transfers above Rp1,000,000 require two recorded admin participants; an ordinary payout up to that amount requires Finance Admin re-authentication | Separation of duties | Missing approval keeps the financial action disabled |
| `UR-BR-041` | Every payout/refund/split leg receives a unique operation ID before manual transfer and has `PROCESSING`, `SUCCESS`, `FAILED`, or `UNKNOWN` result | Financial idempotency | No terminal state or new attempt exists without a recorded operation |
| `UR-BR-042` | An `UNKNOWN` transfer must be reconciled before retry; only a failed leg may be retried, and evidence corrections append a new audit event rather than overwriting history | Financial recovery | Blind retry and mutation of successful evidence are forbidden |
| `UR-BR-043` | Manual operations run 09:00-21:00 WIB daily; payment review target is two operating hours, payout target is 1x24 hours after eligibility, and refund/split target is 2x24 hours after approval | Operating SLA | Delay is visible to participants and escalated without pretending completion |
| `UR-BR-044` | BayarAman in-app status is the source of truth; an automatic notification is attempted at most three times and final failure is visible to admin | Notifications | Failed delivery never changes transaction state or authorizes a manual/financial action |
| `UR-BR-045` | Participant-shared financial views show at most bank name and last four account digits; full values are limited to the owner and authorized Finance/Supervisor access | Sensitive-data visibility | Unauthorized access is denied and audited |
| `UR-BR-046` | A real-money pilot cannot launch until Midtrans merchant settlement, custody/forwarding arrangement, consumer disclosures, complaint handling, data controls, credentials, webhook deployment, and legal/compliance review are validated | Launch readiness | Product remains prototype/test-only until the gate is recorded |
| `UR-BR-047` | Cancellation eligibility is determined from requester role, current state/version, payment exposure, shipment checkpoint, risk hold, and financial-processing cutoff | Cancellation entry | Unauthorized or stale request is rejected and audited without changing transaction state |
| `UR-BR-048` | Submitting a cancellation request is distinct from approving, completing, refunding, or otherwise financially resolving it | Cancellation lifecycle | UI/status cannot present a request as a terminal result |
